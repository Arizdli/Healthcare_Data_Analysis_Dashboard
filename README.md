# Healthcare Patient Analytics Dashboard
## Project Objective
An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes patient demographics, diagnoses, treatment plans and key health indicators (heart rate, blood pressure, cholesterol, BMI) for 500 patients between 01-01-2024 and 14-05-2025.

## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/Arizdli/Healthcare_Data_Analysis_Dashboard/blob/main/healthcare_dataset.csv">healthcare_dataset</a>

## Dataset Questions
- How many total patients are in the dataset?
- What is the gender split of patients (Male, Female, Other)?
- Which age group has the highest and lowest number of patients?
- What is the average age of patients for each diagnosis?
- What is the distribution of patients across each diagnosis?
- Which treatment plan is used most often and which least often?

## KPIs Questions
- What are the average heart rate, blood pressure and cholesterol across all patients?
- Which diagnosis has the highest average blood pressure?
- Do average heart rate, BMI and cholesterol differ by diagnosis or gender?
- Which months have the highest and lowest patient counts?
- How do patient volumes compare across 2024 and 2025?
